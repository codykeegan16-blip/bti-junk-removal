Quick answers

      

        
How do you set the price?
By how much of our 5×8 trailer your stuff fills. Send a photo and we text back a price. You approve it before we load.

        
Do I have to carry anything out?
No. Point at it and we carry it out, from anywhere on the property.

        
What won't you take?
Paint, chemicals, fuel, propane tanks, asbestos and other hazardous waste. Ask us if you're not sure.

        
Any extra charges?
Only if they apply: +$25 per flight of stairs, +$40 same-day, +$15 per tire. Dirt, rock, concrete and roofing are priced from a photo because they're heavy.

        
Do I need to be home?
Not always. If the stuff is outside or in an open garage, tell us in your text and we'll sort it out.
